Overprint

Screen-printed posters, pulled by hand.

Most print filters lay a flat duotone over your photo. Overprint builds the poster the way a real shop pulls it: it separates the image by tone, screens each ink as its own halftone, overprints two inks so their overlap multiplies into a third color, and lets the paper show through.

Made for gig-poster and sticker artists, zine makers, and anyone who loves the look of real ink on paper, without the ink under their nails.

What it does

  • Import any photo and Overprint auto-levels it onto the ink, so bright subjects read instead of washing out to bare paper.
  • Real halftone screening with per-ink angles, so the inks interleave instead of muddying, exactly like a physical press.
  • Overprint two inks and the overlap multiplies into a third color, the effect the app is named for.
  • Set text in heavy poster type and drag it anywhere on the sheet.
  • Warm paper stocks with real tooth, from bright white to midnight.

Pull by hand

Grab the squeegee and pull. Your speed and dwell become the ink body and gradient, heavy where you pull slow and thin where you pull fast, so no two pulls are the same. Split-fountain and gradient pulls roll one ink into another across the sheet.

Export, no watermark

Share a clean, web-ready poster for free. No watermark, ever. Overprint Plus unlocks the full print shop: full-resolution files, one file per ink separation for the printer, a proof PDF with registration and crop marks, and apparel mockups.

Private by design

Everything runs on your device. Your photos never leave it. No account, no cloud, no analytics, and no tracking. Overprint Plus is a one-time purchase, yours forever, with Family Sharing and a free Restore.
