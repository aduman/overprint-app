Privacy Policy

Overprint

Last updated 8 July 2026

Overprint does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account, no cloud, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

All editing happens locally on your iPhone or iPad. The photos you open and the posters you create never leave your device. Your saved presses, palettes, and settings are stored only on your device.

Photo library

Overprint asks for permission to add photos to your library only so it can save a poster you export. It does not read or upload your photo library. You choose each photo to open through the system photo picker, and the original stays on your device.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Overprint Plus" unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Overprint never sees your payment information. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and can be restored for free.

Data collection

None. Overprint collects no usage data and contains no third-party SDKs, advertising, or trackers. This matches the "Data Not Collected" label on the App Store.

Children

Overprint is rated 4+, is suitable for all ages, and collects no data from anyone.
