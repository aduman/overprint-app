Frequently asked

How do I make a poster?

Import a photo, add inks from the ink rail, then grab the squeegee and pull to lay the ink down. Fine-tune with Adjust (dot size, screen angle, registration, paper), place text if you want it, then Export.

What is in Overprint Plus?

A one-time purchase that unlocks the full press: 3 to 6 spot inks (free tops out at 2), premium metallic and fluoro inks, manual press controls including split-fountain and gradient pulls, and print-grade export with full resolution, per-ink separations, and a proof PDF. No subscription.

What are the separations and proof PDF for?

If you print on a real screen-printing or risograph setup, the export gives each ink as its own file plus a proof PDF with registration and crop marks, ready to send to a printer.

Do my photos leave my device?

No. Everything runs on your device. See the privacy policy.

I bought Plus on another device.

Open Settings inside Overprint and tap Restore Purchases. Plus is tied to your Apple ID and is Family Shareable.
